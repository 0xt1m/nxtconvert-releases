How to convert DOCX to HTML

  2. Add your DOCX filesDrag them onto the window, or click Browse files. You can add as many as you like.

  4. ConvertPress Convert. Your HTML files appear in Downloads/nxtconvert, and the originals stay untouched.

DOCX and HTML

DOCX is the Microsoft Word format, the most common way to share editable documents.

HTML is the language of web pages, which makes it easy to publish or reuse document content online.

Documents keep their headings, lists, tables and images where the target format supports them.

Why convert on your computer?

Online converters upload your DOCX files to someone else’s server, limit file sizes and often add queues or watermarks. nxtconvert does the work locally with the same engines professional tools use, so it’s private, works offline and handles large files and big batches.
